A note about problem statements vs. user stories …
Problem Statement: This should live at the top of your epic and should use the How Might We format. It’s meant to widen the scope and get the team in a problem solving headspace rather than a “build what someone requested” headspace. We use this at the epic level instead of a user story because it’s limiting to expect that one POV can guide a whole project.
User Story: This should live at the ticket level. Every ticket should have at least one user story at the top so that when you open the ticket, you know what the person is working on and why it’s important if you aren’t on that team. These have a narrow view and represent a smaller slice of what we’re building than a How Might We problem statement does. 
Tasks: These are nested in a ticket / user story to help engineering make the ticket’s acceptance criteria happen.
